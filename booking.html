While it’s apart… brake pad check?

Would you like brake pads with that? While the bike or suspension is here, we can check pad wear and let you know before replacing anything.

Leaking fork oil or shock oil can contaminate brake pads. Once brake pads are oil-contaminated, stopping power can be reduced and the contaminated pad material can also increase brake disc wear. If we see any sign of oil contamination, we will contact you before replacing pads or recommending new parts.

We will not replace brake pads without confirming price and approval first. Replacement options are quote first only — no automatic pricing.
