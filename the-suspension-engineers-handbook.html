Frequently asked questions

About The Suspension Engineer's Handbook

What is The Suspension Engineer's Handbook?

It is a motorcycle suspension engineering book covering damping, shim stacks, rebound control, dyno interpretation, troubleshooting, tyre interaction, and whole-bike balance.

Who is this suspension book for?

It is written for suspension tuners, racers, technicians, engineers, and advanced riders who want a more structured understanding of motorcycle suspension.

Is this a beginner suspension setup guide?

It is more detailed than a basic clicker guide. It is built as a practical handbook that connects workshop decisions with engineering reasoning.

Does the book cover shim stack design?

Yes. It includes shim stack design logic, practical valving strategy, crossover stacks, ring shims, and rebound/compression thinking.

Does the book cover dyno interpretation?

Yes. It includes force-velocity thinking, dyno reading, troubleshooting, and trackside development logic.
