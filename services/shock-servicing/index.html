What the service covers

Know what you are booking

A shock service includes fluids, oil and dust seal, guide bush, O-rings, cleaning and inspection. Gas pressure and setup are checked for the component. Damaged shafts or other wear items are quoted before repair. A kicking rear end may indicate service condition, spring mismatch, rebound settings or chassis balance.

A$340 for a loose component; A$460 with the complete bike. For loose work, arrange delivery or Canberra collection in advance. The bike's make, model and year help confirm the component and any unusual parts needs.

Can a kicking shock need more than service?

Yes. A worn shock can lose consistency, while spring rate, sag and damping settings also affect kick and traction. The workshop checks condition and setup before recommending revalving.

For both forks and shock supplied loose together, the standard combined service is A$600. See the complete price list and inclusions.
