What is included

A proper baseline—not random clicker changes

Measure

Rider sag, static sag, bike balance, fork height and current suspension settings.

Diagnose

Suspension health, tyre pressures and the likely causes of harshness, kicking, diving, deflection, poor grip or bottoming.

Set up & explain

Baseline clickers, practical adjustments and a written Suspension Health Report showing what to do now, later or simply monitor.
