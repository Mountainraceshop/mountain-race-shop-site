Air Spring Progression vs Coil Springs

Matching sag is not the same as matching the entire spring force curve.

Why air springs ramp up

For a simplified sealed positive chamber, absolute pressure follows P = P₀(V₀/V)ⁿ. Slow compression with heat exchange approaches n = 1; rapid compression with negligible heat exchange approaches n = 1.4 for air. Actual behaviour depends on heat transfer and operating conditions.

What changes with a coil conversion?

A linear coil spring adds force in proportion to displacement (F = F₀ + kx). An ideal positive air chamber has a rising incremental stiffness as volume falls. Actual fork force also depends on negative chambers, effective piston areas, trapped outer-chamber air, friction and hydraulic bottoming systems. Pressure alone is not total fork force.

Workshop implications

Compare force versus travel, not just sag. Choose spring support for rider mass and application, then tune compression and rebound damping and bottoming control separately. Neither spring type is automatically superior. Follow manufacturer pressure and service limits.
