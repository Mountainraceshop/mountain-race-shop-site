When revalving is the right job

A change in shim-stack design can alter damping response, but it should follow a spring and service review. Craig starts with rider weight without gear, motorcycle make/model/year, riding type and the actual handling complaint. A fork that feels harsh yet still bottoms might need a different spring, oil-height check, or damping direction; the symptom alone does not identify the valve change.

The standard package covers fork and shock servicing, revalving, springs and rider-specific setup. For air forks, the off-bike A$1,080 package does not include fork springs; adding shock service brings the off-bike package to A$1,260. Compare the package inclusions.

Can I request revalving alone?

Tell Craig what work has already been done and what the bike does now. He will confirm the scope and price before work begins rather than treating a clicker complaint as proof that new valving is required.
