About Suspension Tuning Made Simple

What is Suspension Tuning Made Simple?

It is a plain-English motorcycle suspension setup guide covering springs, sag, preload, compression, rebound, oil height, tyre pressure, clickers, testing methods, and when to get a revalve.

Who is this suspension guide for?

It is written for riders, home mechanics, and workshop staff who want to understand what their motorcycle suspension is telling them and make practical setup decisions.

Is this book technical or beginner-friendly?

It is written in plain English without unnecessary jargon. It is designed to be accessible to riders of all experience levels while still being practical and useful.

What riding styles does it cover?

It is ideal for motocross, enduro, road, track, adventure, and general motorcycle riders who want better control, confidence, comfort, and grip.

Does it explain how to fix common suspension problems?

Yes. It helps riders translate common complaints like harshness, kicking, packing, diving, wallowing, poor grip, and nervous handling into practical setup decisions.

What is the difference between this and The Suspension Engineer's Handbook?

The Suspension Engineer's Handbook is a deeper, more technical reference covering shim stack design, dyno interpretation, damping calculations, and engineering methodology. Suspension Tuning Made Simple is a practical, plain-English guide focused on setup fundamentals, troubleshooting, and making better decisions at the bike.
