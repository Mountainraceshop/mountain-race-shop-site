What this motorcycle suspension book covers

The Suspension Engineer's Handbook is a practical motorcycle suspension engineering book written for tuners, racers, suspension technicians, and serious riders who want to move beyond guesswork. It takes the reader from spring rates, wheel rates, and damping fundamentals through to shim stack design, rebound control, dyno interpretation, cavitation, tyre interaction, troubleshooting, and whole-bike balance.

This is not a generic clicker guide. It is a structured handbook built to explain why the bike behaves the way it does, how to calculate what matters, and how to turn rider complaints into disciplined engineering decisions.

Shim Stack Design

  • Piston design, hydraulic flow paths, and gas pressure behaviour

Diagnostics & Development
