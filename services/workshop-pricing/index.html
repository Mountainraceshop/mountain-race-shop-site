Pricing notes

What is and is not fixed-price

Suspension services above are fixed for standard serviceable motorcycle components in normal condition. Brake-pad and steering-head fixed add-on pricing applies to motocross and enduro motorcycles only.

Adventure, road, trail and other motorcycles are quoted first because access, bodywork, bearing-kit cost and brake configuration can materially change labour and parts cost. We do not fit additional parts without confirming the price first.
