Suspension that works — not guesswork

A setup should give you traction, support and confidence

A good suspension setup does more than make a bike feel “softer” or “firmer.” It gives you better traction, better stability, better hold-up in the right part of the stroke, and more confidence when the speed goes up or the terrain gets rough.

At Mountain Race Shop, every recommendation is built around what the bike is actually doing — not generic settings pulled from a chart. Workshop experience is combined with structured analysis to create setups that are repeatable, understandable and aimed at real performance.
