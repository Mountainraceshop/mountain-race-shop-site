Which book should I start with?

Start with Suspension Tuning Made Simple for practical setup, clicker adjustments and troubleshooting in plain English. Choose The Suspension Engineer’s Handbook for the broader suspension engineering reference. Choose Shim Stack Engineering when your focus is calculating and building damping valves for forks and shocks.
