What we actually do

Find the cause, then choose the solution

A harsh fork does not automatically need softer damping. A kicking shock does not automatically need more rebound. We look at spring rate, sag, preload, friction, geometry, tyres, service condition, damping and how the motorcycle is being used.

That may lead to servicing, springs, revalving or setup changes. Sometimes the correct recommendation is to change nothing at all.

Start with the symptoms

Send the bike make, model and year, rider weight without gear, riding type and a plain-English description of what the bike is doing: harsh, kicking, diving, deflecting, running wide, bottoming or not holding a line.
