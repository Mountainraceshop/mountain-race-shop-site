# Mountain Race Shop™ Internal AI Usage Policy

**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective date:** 19 July 2026  
**Policy owner:** Mountain Race Shop  
**Review cycle:** Annually and whenever a major AI system is introduced

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets the rules for using artificial intelligence within Mountain Race Shop while protecting customer information, workshop data, intellectual property and engineering integrity.

AI is a support tool. It does not replace professional judgement, technician accountability or established workshop records.

## 2. Core rule

> AI assists us. It never replaces our judgement.

All customer-facing communication, pricing, technical recommendations, suspension specifications and engineering decisions must be checked by an authorised person before use.

## 3. Approved uses

AI may be used for:

- drafting customer emails and booking responses;
- website, marketing and social-media content;
- internal procedures, checklists and training material;
- organising non-sensitive workshop information;
- technical documentation and calculation support;
- spreadsheet and administrative automation;
- summarising approved business records;
- developing software and internal tools;
- preliminary diagnostic thinking, provided a technician makes the final decision.

## 4. Prohibited data

The following must never be entered into an AI system:

- passwords, recovery codes or authentication tokens;
- payment-card information;
- banking details;
- driver's licences, passports or identity documents;
- private keys, API secrets or website credentials;
- medical information unless specifically authorised and genuinely required;
- information that is subject to a confidentiality agreement unless the tool and use have been specifically approved.

## 5. Customer information

Only the minimum information necessary may be used.

Before using customer material with an AI tool, remove or replace where practical:

- customer names;
- phone numbers;
- email addresses;
- street addresses;
- registration numbers;
- VIN or chassis numbers;
- invoice numbers;
- other information that directly identifies the customer.

Use neutral labels such as “Customer A”, “2022 motorcycle” or “Workshop Job 01” where identity is not needed.

## 6. Suspension and engineering data

Rider weight, motorcycle model, service history, suspension settings, dyno results, shim stacks, valving specifications and setup notes are confidential workshop information.

They may be used only when:

- the use is directly related to servicing, diagnosis, engineering or internal improvement;
- identifying details have been removed where possible;
- the selected AI tool is approved;
- the output is reviewed by an experienced technician.

Customer tuning information must not be published or shared externally without permission.

## 7. Intellectual property and supplier information

Do not enter the following into an AI tool unless specifically authorised:

- supplier-confidential documents;
- unpublished designs;
- proprietary software source code held under licence;
- confidential contracts;
- customer-developed products;
- race-team information supplied in confidence;
- commercially sensitive pricing or margin information unless the tool is approved for that purpose.

## 8. Engineering verification

AI output must be treated as unverified until checked.

Before technical information is used, verify:

- units and conversions;
- formula assumptions;
- motorcycle make, model and year;
- spring rates, dimensions and travel;
- shim dimensions and stack order;
- sign conventions;
- dyno data and graph labels;
- suitability for the rider and intended use;
- compatibility with current workshop practice.

No AI-generated setup or valving specification may be installed solely because an AI system recommended it.

## 9. Customer communication verification

Before sending AI-assisted customer communication, check:

- customer name and contact details;
- pricing and inclusions;
- workshop availability;
- dates and pickup arrangements;
- technical claims;
- spelling and tone;
- links and attachments;
- that no other customer's information is included.

## 10. Approved tools register

Mountain Race Shop will maintain a simple register containing:

- tool name;
- business purpose;
- account owner;
- data permitted;
- data prohibited;
- security settings;
- date approved;
- next review date.

A new AI service must not be used with customer or confidential workshop information until it has been assessed.

## 11. Access and account security

- Use individual accounts where practical.
- Enable multi-factor authentication where available.
- Do not share passwords.
- Remove access promptly when no longer required.
- Keep operating systems, browsers and applications updated.
- Store official workshop records in approved business systems, not only in AI conversation history.

## 12. Records and retention

AI systems are not the official record of customer work.

Final approved information must be saved in the relevant booking, customer, job, invoice or workshop record. Temporary AI conversations containing business information should be deleted when no longer required where the platform allows.

## 13. Incident response

An AI or data incident includes:

- customer information entered into the wrong tool;
- confidential information exposed to an unauthorised person;
- an AI output sent with incorrect pricing or technical information;
- compromised AI account credentials;
- customer data appearing in another customer's communication.

When an incident occurs:

1. Stop the affected use immediately.
2. Preserve enough information to understand what occurred.
3. Change credentials or restrict access where necessary.
4. Identify the information and people affected.
5. Correct or withdraw inaccurate customer communication.
6. Notify the business owner promptly.
7. Assess whether customers, service providers or regulators must be notified.
8. Record the incident and prevention action.

## 14. Staff and contractor responsibilities

Anyone using AI for Mountain Race Shop must:

- follow this policy;
- use approved tools only;
- minimise customer data;
- verify outputs;
- report mistakes or suspected breaches promptly;
- ask before using AI where confidentiality or risk is unclear.

## 15. Annual review checklist

At least once each year, review:

- the approved tools register;
- user access and multi-factor authentication;
- customer-data handling practices;
- whether public website statements remain accurate;
- any incidents or near misses;
- technical verification procedures;
- new legal, privacy or platform requirements;
- whether any tool should be removed or replaced.

## 16. Public commitment

The public AI and Customer Privacy Policy must accurately reflect actual workshop practice. Marketing statements must not claim security, certification or legal compliance that Mountain Race Shop cannot substantiate.
