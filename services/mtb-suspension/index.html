Diagnose before changing parts

What we look for

Harshness can come from excessive damping, insufficient damping, friction, pressure, setup or component condition. A fork that dives, a shock that packs or a bike that feels dead over repeated hits needs the cause identified before money is spent.

Diagnose. Engineer. Tune. Teach.

Harsh / poor small-bump response

Check pressure, damping, friction, seals, bushings and service condition.

Diving / blowing through travel

Check air-spring support, volume, compression control and rider setup.

Packing / loss of grip

Check rebound recovery, heat, pressure and shock condition.
