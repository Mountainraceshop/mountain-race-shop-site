# Mountain Race Shop — Revised Business Plan & SWOT

**Updated: August 2026**

## Strategic direction

Mountain Race Shop should not respond to new suspension competitors by becoming cheaper, broader or larger. The business should move further toward specialist diagnosis, engineering, setup and education.

Core position:

**Diagnose → Engineer → Tune → Teach**

The objective is to own the specialist diagnosis and engineering position across Canberra/ACT, Snowy Monaro and the South Coast rather than compete for generic suspension servicing.

## Confirmed market changes

- **Hume:** Suspension Smith is a confirmed specialist motorcycle suspension competitor operating from Unit 44/23 Paspaley St, Hume, ACT, with an established social audience and road/dirt/adventure positioning.
- **Beard:** specialist suspension competition is present in the Canberra market.
- **Bega Valley:** motorcycle workshops and dealerships provide local suspension capability, increasing local competition for routine work.
- **Bombala:** Bombala Cycles & Chainsaws has publicly announced its final trading day as 31 August 2026, creating an immediate service gap in Bombala and the southern Monaro.

## Geographic priorities

### 1. Bombala / Delegate / southern Snowy Monaro — immediate acquisition opportunity

Launch a regional continuity campaign from 1 September 2026 without referring negatively to the closing business.

Message:

> Specialist motorcycle suspension servicing for Bombala, Delegate and the Monaro — fork and shock servicing, springs, revalving and handling diagnosis from Mountain Race Shop.

Actions:

- Publish the Bombala/Snowy Monaro landing page.
- Contact Bombala Cycles & Chainsaws privately if the relationship permits and offer to look after suspension customers after closure.
- Ask whether specialist tools, parts stock or motorcycle workshop assets are being sold.
- Test grouped Bombala/Delegate collection only when pre-booked work makes the run commercially viable.
- Prefer loose suspension for remote customers where practical.

Initial target: acquire 10–15 new southern Monaro customers during the first 30–60 days after closure without discounting workshop labour.

### 2. Canberra / ACT — defend through differentiation and convenience

Confirmed specialist competition in Hume means generic “we do suspension” advertising is no longer enough.

Mountain Race Shop should defend Canberra through:

- Monday Canberra pickup.
- $50 Setup Saturdays.
- structured booking and enquiry intake.
- Post-Race Suspension Report.
- published technical articles.
- The Suspension Engineer's Handbook.
- Shim Calculator.
- case studies and measurable rider outcomes.

Do not enter price competition. Make the difference visible before a customer commits to a major job.

### 3. Bega Valley / South Coast — selective specialist growth

Do not compete with dealerships or general workshops for routine servicing. Position Mountain Race Shop as the specialist for jobs worth sending away:

- difficult harshness/deflection complaints.
- spring-rate corrections.
- revalving.
- race suspension.
- adventure-bike load setup.
- bikes that have already been serviced but still do not handle correctly.

Test grouped collection runs only after demand is proven.

## SWOT — August 2026

### Strengths
- specialist motorcycle suspension experience dating to 1995.
- engineering-led diagnosis rather than parts replacement.
- proprietary and original assets: Handbook, Shim Calculator, Technical Series, Post-Race Report and enquiry workflow.
- low fixed-overhead workshop base.
- Canberra access without Canberra commercial rent.
- customer education as part of the service.

### Weaknesses
- heavy dependence on Craig for engineering, workshop delivery, sales and technical content.
- limited workshop throughput for high-value specialist work.
- Shannons Flat can appear less convenient than Hume/Canberra competitors.
- production booking/database workflow is still being completed.

### Opportunities
- confirmed Bombala closure creates an immediate service gap.
- new Canberra competition validates the size of the suspension market.
- Bega Valley/South Coast off-road and adventure market.
- growing adventure-bike load/setup demand.
- scalable education revenue through books, software and future training.
- AI-assisted administration and structured job data.
- regional collection and postal servicing where commercially justified.

### Threats
- specialist competitors in Hume/Canberra reduce the convenience advantage.
- price discounting from new businesses attempting to gain market share.
- key-person risk.
- competitors copying generic “engineering” or “data-driven” marketing language.
- over-expansion across too many regions and projects at once.

## 90-day priorities

1. **Own the Bombala service gap** with a dedicated page, quiet regional campaign and referral conversation where appropriate.
2. **Defend Canberra** with Monday pickup, Setup Saturdays, technical authority and structured diagnosis.
3. **Establish Bega Valley visibility** with a dedicated specialist landing page before committing to collection logistics.
4. **Finish production booking automation** so enquiry → intake → booking → job record → follow-up requires minimal duplicate admin.
5. **Continue Technical Series publishing** every 1–2 weeks and connect every article to the Handbook, Shim Calculator and workshop booking path.

## Twelve-month target

By August 2027 Mountain Race Shop should be recognised as the specialist motorcycle suspension engineering business for Canberra, Snowy Monaro and the South Coast — with workshop services, technical publications and engineering tools that are difficult for competitors to reproduce.

The goal is not to be the largest workshop. The goal is to be the workshop serious riders seek out when ordinary servicing has not solved the handling problem.
