The Mountain Race Shop approach

We don't start with the modification.

We start with the rider complaint: harshness, kicking, diving, deflection, poor grip, fatigue or a bike that simply will not hold a line.

From there we work through sag, spring suitability, service condition, chassis balance and damping before recommending parts or internal changes.

Diagnose. Engineer. Tune. Teach.

Diagnose

Find the real cause rather than chasing clickers.

Engineer

Choose the minimum change that solves the actual problem.

Tune

Build a usable baseline for rider, bike and terrain.

Teach

Leave understanding what changed and why.

Don't leave it until race week.

Allow enough time for inspection, servicing, parts supply and any setup changes before 24–25 October. If you are unsure what the bike needs, start with diagnosis rather than ordering parts blindly.
