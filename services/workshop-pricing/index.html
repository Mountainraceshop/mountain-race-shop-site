MX / Enduro add-ons

Steering-head bearings and brakes

These workshop add-ons are available for motocross and enduro motorcycles. Adventure, road, trail and other motorcycles are call-and-quote because access, bodywork, caliper design and steering-head labour vary significantly.

MX / Enduro steering-head bearing check & adjustment

Check free play/notchiness, adjust preload where serviceable and report condition.

MX / Enduro steering-head bearing clean & regrease

Strip sufficiently to clean, inspect, grease and correctly adjust reusable bearings.

MX / Enduro steering-head bearing replacement

Remove old bearings/races, fit and grease replacement bearings, set preload and recheck steering. Unusually expensive or non-standard kits are confirmed first.

Brake pad inspection

Included with suspension service

Pad thickness and obvious oil contamination checked while the motorcycle is in the workshop.

MX / Enduro brake pad replacement

Remove, inspect and clean caliper contact areas, fit pads and verify operation. Pad price is confirmed before fitting.
