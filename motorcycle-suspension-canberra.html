Find out why the bike feels wrong

Harshness, deflection, kicking, packing, bottoming and vague handling can come from springs, damping, friction, geometry, tyres, service condition or chassis balance. We diagnose the system rather than selling a predetermined part.

Getting suspension to the workshop

Canberra pickup and drop-off is available on Mondays with a confirmed workshop booking. Tell us whether you have a complete bike or loose forks and shock, and we will confirm the collection point and timing before you travel. The workshop itself is at Shannons Flat, NSW.

For Canberra riders who cannot get away midweek, the practical first step is to describe the symptom and book the transport or workshop slot. Harshness over braking bumps, front-end deflection and a rear that kicks can have different causes; Craig will ask about the surface, speed, recent servicing and current settings before suggesting a job.

Common booking questions

How much is motorcycle fork servicing?

A standard fork service is A$320 when the forks are supplied loose, or A$360 with the complete bike. Oil and dust seals are included; additional wear parts or repairs are confirmed before fitting.

Can you service both forks and shock?

Yes. Standard fork and shock servicing together is A$600 when both are supplied loose. A shock service alone is A$340 loose or A$460 on the bike.

Do you pick up in Canberra?

Monday Canberra pickup and drop-off is available with a confirmed workshop booking. Contact Craig to confirm the collection point, capacity and timing; do not assume collection in other towns.

Can I send loose suspension?

Yes, by arrangement. Contact Craig before sending forks or a shock so the components, return details and workshop timing can be confirmed.
