Bombala and Delegate service details

Getting suspension to the workshop

Riders in Bombala and Delegate can arrange to bring a complete bike to Shannons Flat or send loose forks and shock by prior agreement. Confirm timing and the work requested before travelling or dispatching parts.

If the bike deflects on rocky sections or loses drive over chop, include the current spring and clicker settings if known. A service checks component condition first; spring and revalve decisions follow the rider, terrain and symptoms.

Common booking questions

How much is motorcycle fork servicing?

A standard fork service is A$320 when the forks are supplied loose, or A$360 with the complete bike. Oil and dust seals are included; additional wear parts or repairs are confirmed before fitting.

Can you service both forks and shock?

Yes. Standard fork and shock servicing together is A$600 when both are supplied loose. A shock service alone is A$340 loose or A$460 on the bike.

Do you pick up in Canberra?

Monday Canberra pickup and drop-off is available with a confirmed workshop booking. Contact Craig to confirm the collection point, capacity and timing; do not assume collection in other towns.

Can I send loose suspension?

Yes, by arrangement. Contact Craig before sending forks or a shock so the components, return details and workshop timing can be confirmed.
