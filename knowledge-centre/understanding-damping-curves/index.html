Suspension Engineering

Understanding Damping Curves

Damping curves show force versus velocity and help translate hardware changes into behavior.

Try Shim CalculatorBuy the HandbookBook Service

A curve does not replace rider feedback, but it gives feedback a technical map.

Why it matters

Motorcycle suspension problems rarely come from one isolated setting. Rider weight, spring rate, geometry, oil condition, bleed, valving and tyre behavior all interact. The useful question is not whether the setting is softer or harder; it is what part of the stroke, speed range or chassis attitude needs to change.

How to think about the change

Start with the symptom, then decide whether the correction belongs in service condition, spring setup, clicker range, oil height, shim stack design or a broader chassis balance change. That sequence prevents random adjustments and makes each test easier to understand.

Workshop checklist

  • Record current sag, clickers and rider feedback before changing parts.
  • Confirm service condition before blaming the shim stack.
  • Change one meaningful variable at a time.
  • Use repeatable terrain or test sections when judging direction.
